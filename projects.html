Projects

Tools for deal teams, built on live deals.

Most of these started as a frustration at 11 p.m. with a CIM open. Each one does a narrow job well, and leaves the judgment with people.

01AI workflows

  • Deal-to-Script

    Live

    A pre-meeting briefing agent. It turns scattered deal materials into two decision-ready outputs for a senior stakeholder: a one-page investment memo and a six-scene, 60–90 second video script. The goal is to walk into a meeting prepared in under two minutes.

    1. InputsCIM excerpt, IC notes, deal summary, news
    2. ExtractFacts, drivers, risks, and the thesis
    3. EmitOne-page memo plus scene-by-scene script
    4. QA gateSchema check on scenes, timing, word limits
    Stack
    Claude (system-prompt workflow) · JSON Schema · PowerShell validator
    Example
    Worked case on a sample SaaS CIM: validation pass at 75 seconds
    Why
    The analysis and scripting is the defensible part; rendering stays tool-agnostic

  • CIM red-flag reviewer

    Building

    A first-pass read of a CIM against a standard diligence checklist. It returns a red / amber / green table with page references, so the first team call starts with questions instead of a summary.

    Stack
    Claude · PDF extraction · Excel output
    Guardrail
    Every flag cites a page; nothing is asserted without a source

  • Comps commentary drafter

    Concept

    Takes a trading comps table and drafts the two paragraphs everyone writes by hand: where the target sits, why, and what would move it.

    Stack
    Python · Claude · Capital IQ export

  • Earnings call digest

    Concept

    A Monday-morning brief on portfolio-relevant public comps: guidance changes, tone shifts, and the three quotes worth reading.

    Stack
    Transcript feed · Claude · email delivery
